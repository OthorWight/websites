Two ways to play

Standard Sudoku

Fill every row, column, and 3×3 box with 1–9, using each digit once. Printed givens stay fixed.

Killer Sudoku

All Standard rules apply. Each dashed cage has a total in its top-left cell. The cage’s digits must add up to that total, and a digit cannot repeat within a cage, even across boxes. A one-cell cage tells you that cell’s digit. Generated puzzles include fixed starting digits: 24 on Easy, 16 on Normal, 10 on Hard, and 5 on Expert.

Select a cage to see its remaining total and possible combinations. Those combinations also account for the filled digits and row, column, and box restrictions.

Pencil notes & assistance

Notes toggles pencil marks instead of digits. Auto notes calculates candidates for the current board, including cage sums. Highlight singles marks one-candidate cells and digits with only one position in a row, column, or box. Check compares your entries with the solution. Hint fills a logical single when possible, or reveals one cell if there is no single available.

Enter a puzzle from your Android game

Choose its type, then Enter your own puzzle. Enter printed givens. For Killer, choose Draw cages, drag across connected cells, type the total, and press Enter to add the cage. Clicking a cell starts a fresh selection; Shift-click adds or removes individual cells. You can also use Add cage. Backspace corrects the total; Escape clears the selection. Delete removes the selected cage; Undo restores it. Every cell must belong to exactly one cage. To change a cage, select it, remove it, then redraw it. Validate & play checks the rules and confirms a single solution.

Difficulty presets control clue counts and cage sizes; they are approximate, not formal technique ratings. Generated puzzles are verified to have one solution. Timer counts active play while this tab is visible. There is no three-mistake limit.
